# research task

Weekly research pass on the dubwise sound.

1. Read `AGENTS.md`, `skills/dubwise/SKILL.md` and the riddim notation in
   `skills/dubwise/scripts/notation.mjs`.
2. Pick one concrete idea that would make the app sound or feel more like a real dub sound
   system: a riddim structure, a bassline movement, a delay or reverb convention, an arrangement
   detail, or a scene reference worth adding.
3. Prove the idea is valid with the skill CLI (`spec`, `check`), and paste the arguable part of
   the output into the issue.
4. Open at most two issues with `gh issue create`: the idea, why it matters musically, and the
   smallest implementation step.
5. Never edit code, never open a PR. If nothing is worth doing this week, open nothing and say so.
